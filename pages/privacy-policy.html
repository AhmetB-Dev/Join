2. Data Processing

JOIN processes personal data only where necessary to provide and operate the application.

When creating an account, information such as your name, email address and password is processed. Passwords are not stored in plain text.

JOIN also processes data created within the application, including contacts, tasks, due dates, assigned contacts, subtasks and related task information.

The legal basis for processing required to provide the application and user account is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.

3. Authentication and Browser Storage

JOIN uses local storage and session storage for technically necessary functions such as authentication, guest access and temporary application state.

This information is not used for advertising, analytics or cross-site tracking.

Where browser storage is strictly necessary to provide functionality expressly requested by the user, Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG applies.

4. Technical Data

When JOIN or its backend is accessed, technically necessary data may be processed, including the IP address, time of access, browser information and requested resources.

This processing is necessary for the secure and reliable operation of JOIN. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

5. Storage Period

Personal data is stored only for as long as necessary for the respective purpose or as required by law.

Account-related data is generally stored while the corresponding user account exists. Tasks and contacts are stored until they are deleted or the associated account is removed.

6. Recipients

Personal data is not sold and is not provided to third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Technical service providers may process data where this is necessary for the operation and hosting of JOIN.

7. Your Rights

Subject to the applicable legal requirements, you have the right to request access to your personal data, correction of inaccurate data, deletion, restriction of processing and data portability.

Where processing is based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, you may also have the right to object to the processing.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority.

For privacy-related requests, contact: ahmet_ba@web.de

8. Automated Decision-Making

JOIN does not use personal data for advertising, profiling or automated decision-making within the meaning of Article 22 GDPR.
